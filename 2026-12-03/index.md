## About
The _Biofeedback & Neurofeedback Community Nijmegen (BNCNijmegen)_ invites you to our next meeting!

**Computational modelling of bio- and neurofeedback learning**  
*hosted by Floris Klumpers*  

Bio- and neurofeedback learning are typically conceptualized as forms of reinforcement learning.
Despite the centrality of this assumption, empirical evidence is lacking,
highlighting the need for computational approaches to formally characterize how feedback
learning evolves (e.g. see Lubianiker et al. 2022).
This meeting will bring together early efforts to understand bio- and neurofeedback
learning through computational modelling.
Dongni Yan (PhD student from Maastricht University) will present her initial explorations
attempting to capture learning dynamics in fMRI neurofeedback. Jonathan Posthuma
(PhD student from Radboud University) will present his preliminary work aimed at
characterizing learning in heart rate variability biofeedback.
The presentations will be followed by an integrative discussion on the opportunities
and challenges of computational approaches to feedback learning.

The meeting is _free_ and _open for everyone_, from students to PIs. We look forward to seeing you there!
